**1 · 80 days (15 s)**
"We're SportiQue, and this is Vault Agent. Eighty days. That's the median wait to move one dataset between institutions on a major clinical data-sharing platform. Thirty-five of those days are the contract alone. The data was ready. The paperwork wasn't."

**2 · Why (15 s)**
"The bottleneck isn't the data. It's people: terms negotiated by email, every person approving every item every round, quality checked only after paying, and card payouts with fees."

**3 · Agents (15 s)**
"So we put an agent on each side. Her agent reads her vault and her rules, runs four checks, and then trades alone, asks her, or blocks with a reason. Let me show you both products."

**4 · Product 1, Clip A plays (37 s clip + 8 s after)**
"Product one, the Data Vault Market. Each owner's agent lists what it will sell, on her terms. Buyer agents arrive with a need and a budget, and negotiate one deal at a time, live, every turn with its reason. Offers that miss the owner's terms are not sold. Each deal is checked, sealed to the buyer, and paid on Mastercard AP4M. Here's the receipt: checks passed, AP4M voucher redeemed, key released." *(clip ends)* "In our fifty-account simulation, eighty of a hundred and nine paid deals closed agent to agent."

**5 · Product 2, Clip B plays (60 s)**
"Product two is ours. SportiQue runs UniQdata for participants and UniQLab for researchers. On the left, a participant joins a study and signs once with her passkey. From then on, her agent shares her daily data by her policy. On the right, the researcher posts sub-projects. The lab's agent finds people who fit, checks each week's data on arrival, and pays rewards automatically. Watch the layers. Daily data flows on its own. Clinical data needs an ethics credential. An MRI request stops and waits for her fresh passkey. A tampered week is caught before payment. In simulation, the approvals a person has to give drop from eleven thousand six hundred to two hundred thirty-three."

**6 · Proof (12 s)**
"And it's real: a live Mastercard AP4M run on XRPL EVM Testnet. Three payouts went through, with receipts verified on-chain. The fourth was over the cap and refused. One channel pays both products."

**7 · Why us (10 s)**
"Others check the buyer's budget. We also check the person's terms. And we built it because we had this problem ourselves."

**8 · Close (8 s)**
"The owner sets the terms. Agents do the deal. AP4M pays. That's Vault Agent. Scan to try it. Thank you."

The same lines are in each slide's speaker notes.
