UniQdata Flutter app (demo build) and participant page · synthetic studies · Testnet: test USD stands in for RLUSD

On the participant side, this is the real UniQdata app. The agree box stays locked until she reads every clause. One passkey signs the relationship with the institution, and each consent is still recorded per study. After that, routine requests go through her agent, and anything new comes back to her. In our simulation of 50 people, column-by-column approvals would have meant 11,600 actions; with her agent and tiered consent it was 233 passkey taps. Response times there are assumed. On the right: her institutions, with skip, pause and withdraw always one tap away.
