We first designed 80 percent on delivery and 20 percent after review. daniel rejected it: if tampered data gets 80 percent, fraud prevention is only worth 20. So provenance is now checked before payment, and the curve rewards staying: 50 percent of a verified week up front, plus 10 points for every consecutive week, up to 90. The rest is held seven days, fourteen more only if fraud is suspected, and paid by default, even after withdrawal. Money already paid is never taken back. Withdrawal history is never an input to pay or priority.
