We start in health research because it is the hardest case: consent, re-identification and withdrawal all apply at once. If the sensitivity layer holds there, it holds for AI training data and robot sensor data with a lighter consent layer. Our year-three estimate is 11.4 million dollars in revenue, under one percent of the roughly 35 thousand trials started each year. On that volume, card fees would be about 3.4 million dollars; on XRPL about 780. A $20 weekly payment costs $0.88 by card and about $0.0002 on XRPL, which is why these small deals never happened before. We start from what buyers already pay for, filling missing person-weeks, instead of collecting supply first, which is where LunaDNA-style ventures failed.
