Here is our contract structure. Between institutions, agents trade data directly. Between an institution and a person, there is one relationship and one consent to the institution's scope. Under it, many sub-projects run: night-shift recovery, blood-pressure follow-up, diet and sleep, sleep apnea imaging. No new contract each time, less admin, less legal work.
