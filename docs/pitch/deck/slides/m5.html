[Clip B plays, 60 s] Product two is ours. SportiQue runs UniQdata for participants and UniQLab for researchers. On the left, a participant joins a study in English and signs once with her passkey; from then on her agent shares her daily data by her policy. On the right, the researcher posts sub-projects, and the lab's agent finds fitting people, checks each week's data on arrival and pays rewards automatically. Watch the layers: daily data flows on its own, clinical data needs an ethics credential, and an MRI request stops and waits for her fresh passkey. A tampered week is caught before payment. Approvals a person has to give drop from eleven thousand six hundred to two hundred thirty-three in simulation.
