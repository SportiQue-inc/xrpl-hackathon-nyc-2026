Data is already traded between institutions and companies, and AI is pulling that market up fast. But one deal is still slow. On one major clinical data-sharing platform the median wait is 80 days, and 35 of those days are just the contract. The data is ready. The paperwork is not. Supply moves only in a few huge deals: Reddit to Google about 60 million a year, News Corp to OpenAI 250 million over five years; only 28 public AI content licences in 2024. Small deals never happen. Why now: public text for AI is running out, people gain rights to pull their own records (TEFCA, EU EHDS 2027, Korea MyData 2027), unconsented supply is getting expensive, and agents can now pay.
