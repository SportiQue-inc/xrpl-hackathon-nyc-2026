(Rivals for Q&A: budget and limit guardrails: Eterna, Fluxyn, Within, AgentVault and at least five more; pay after checking delivery: XGuard, Swanavi, dareskro; closest ledger feature set: XCRMS. Never say "only we"; say "we did not find this combination in the public repos.") Several teams here keep an institution's agent inside its budget and limits, and some pay only after checking delivery. We do that too, through Mastercard AP4M. What we add is a second approval on the other side: the person's own terms. Budget approval is spending authority only; it never authorizes anyone's data. Every other agent here spends money. Ours also turns money down when the person's terms say no.
