Who we are

SportiQue builds research on personal health databases: one person, many studies.

UniQdata

Participant app. People gather their wearable, clinic and survey records in one personal DB and decide which studies get what.

UniQLab

Researcher console. Institutions design studies, request exactly the data they need, and pay per contribution.

One-to-many

One relationship with an institution, many projects under it. No new contract each time.

We are SportiQue. We build a research ecosystem on personal databases. UniQdata is the participant app: people keep their health records in one place. UniQLab is the researcher console. One person, one relationship with an institution, many studies under it.
