And we had the same problem, only harder. In health, every deal touches a person. Consent is tedious: every item, every study, every week. It's hard to judge: consent forms are legal text, and most recruiting posts don't fit you. People drop out, and replacing one costs about 19.5 thousand dollars. And when health data does trade, the person gets nothing: 23andMe's customer DNA went for 305 million dollars in bankruptcy. On top of that, genomes and scans can never be taken back once leaked. That is the wall our UniQdata and UniQLab users hit.
