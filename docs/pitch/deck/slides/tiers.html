So we added a sensitivity layer on top of the marketplace. Every column has a tier. Daily and lifestyle data follow the person's policy and her agent acts alone. Clinical data needs a valid ethics credential. And the top tier, genome, CT, MRI, mental-health diagnoses, is never handled alone and never becomes a standing permission: a fresh passkey every time, even for a top-grade lab. One passkey gives a 15-minute grace for routine approvals. A new consent, a withdrawal, or a change to how she's paid always needs a fresh one. Important: the person never hands over her signature. Her key stays on her phone; the agent only executes what she signed.
