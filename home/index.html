Agents that check, agree and pay for data. The owner stays in control.

Vault Agent lets a buyer's AI agent and a data owner's AI agent settle a data deal in seconds instead of months. Payment runs on Mastercard Agent Pay (AP4M) vouchers on XRPL EVM, under a spending cap the owner sets on chain.

The problem

Data deals are slow, and buyers pay blind

Buying health or research data today means contracts, review boards and invoices passed by hand. Buyers pay before they can see whether the data is any good, and owners lose track of who uses it.

~80 daysfor a typical data deal to close
Up frontbuyers pay before they know the quality
How it works

Three steps, run by agents

  1. Step 1

    The buyer agent asks

    A researcher's or company's agent sends a request: what data, for what purpose, at what price.

  2. Step 2

    The owner agent checks

    It applies the owner's own rules and the data's sensitivity tier. MRI, CT and genomics always need a fresh yes from the person.

    T0 openT1 autoT2 rulesT3 ask the person
  3. Step 3

    Agents pay

    The buyer agent pays with AP4M vouchers, limited by an on-chain spending cap. The owner gets paid on delivery.
