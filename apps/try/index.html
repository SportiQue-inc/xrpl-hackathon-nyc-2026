SportiQue · XRPL Hackathon NYC 2026

Your data. Your rules.
Agents do the work.

SportiQue builds a personal health-data network where each person stores, uses and shares their own records — and can see who used them, for what purpose, and what they received.

It starts with UniQdata, the person's data vault. People collect and review their records, use them for themselves, and choose what to share. UniQLab is one downstream use: a research workspace that receives only the scope a person has approved.

For this hackathon, we built AgentRail: agents find missing data, check a sample, negotiate within owner-set limits, and connect payment release to the key that opens the agreed data. The person keeps control of new and sensitive requests.

01 · Agent trading

A trade is a chain of checks, not a vote.

The owner sets the minimum acceptable price. The buyer sets a budget. Inside those limits, one buyer agent negotiates with one owner agent for each deal.

  1. Find the gap and compare offers New

    Principle
    Ask for a specific missing field, period and quality level.
    Apply
    Open the vault. Inspect an agent recommendation and then one deal in Market activity.
    Watch
    The buyer's need, matching offers and the selected owner's data appear together.
    Result
    You can explain why that owner was selected, rather than counting votes.

  2. Check a sample, then negotiate New

    Principle
    Quality and permission checks happen before funds are committed.
    Apply
    Open a deal's negotiation and protocol steps.
    Watch
    One offer, one counter-offer and the sample checks lead to an agreement or a plain-language reason to stop.
    Result
    The agreed amount is the actual deal amount. A failed sample stops the payment path.

  3. Release payment and open the bundle New

    Principle
    A conditional escrow locks funds. Its release publishes the fulfillment used to recover the data key.
    Apply
    Inspect the paired EscrowCreate and EscrowFinish, then open the purchased bundle in My DB.
    Watch
    The same escrow owner and sequence connect the two receipts. The delivered records retain their source and deal history.
    Result
    A payment receipt and readable data are two distinct outcomes; inspect both.

02 · Healthcare

One person controls the scope. Each request keeps its own record.

In UniQdata, the person controls their records and research contribution. In UniQLab, the researcher defines a study and asks only for the data it needs. AgentRail demonstrates how those requests can be routed and settled.

Demo boundary: the hands-on screens below run the AgentRail engine with synthetic people, demo signing keys and a practice ledger. They demonstrate the workflow; opening them does not prove deployment inside the existing UniQdata or UniQLab products.

  1. Researcher: define the study and co-sign its budget New

    Principle
    Two people, each on their own device, release a study's budget. Budget approval is spending authority only; it never authorizes anyone's data.
    Apply
    Open the UniQLab study console. In Rounds, describe the data gap and choose Propose, then ask both signers.
    Watch
    One signature leaves the budget locked. Two release it in a multi-signed payment.
    Result
    The study can pay for data, and still has no data until people agree.

  2. Person: approve the scope once New

    Principle
    The person approves an institution, purpose, fields and period. Each consent document is read to the end before it can be checked; one signature covers the set, and each consent keeps its own record.
    Apply
    Open your scope and history. Review the institution's scope, read each document to the end and agree.
    Watch
    One relationship with the institution appears, with its sub-projects underneath.
    Result
    Requests inside this scope can be handled by the person's agent. MRI, CT and genomic data are never included.

  3. Person: decide what falls outside the scope New

    Principle
    Automation applies only within the scope already approved. A new field or purpose, and MRI, CT or genomic data, always come back to the person.
    Apply
    Open a contribution request. Read the requested fields and purpose; allow it or choose “Not this one.”
    Watch
    The request becomes allowed or blocked, and the study's routing shows the same outcome.
    Result
    A refusal blocks that request. It is not permission for another sub-project or data type.

  4. Researcher: add a sub-project inside the approved scope New

    Principle
    One institutional relationship covers several sub-projects only within the scope already approved.
    Apply
    In the study console, open Sub-projects and create one with fatigue.survey and heart_rate.daily. Compare it with one that adds an outside-scope field.
    Watch
    The routing summary separates handled inside scope, back to the person, and blocked. Each payment carries the sub-project reference.
    Result
    Approved work proceeds without a new contract each time. A new purpose, field or period needs another decision.

  5. Inspect the contribution and opened data

    Principle
    An approval, a payment and usable data each need their own evidence.
    Apply
    Open a participant's payment history and the matching research contribution. Choose Open bundle after payment release.
    Watch
    Check the item, period, amount, sub-project, release receipt and “Opened · contents match what was sent.” The share paid at release follows a continuity curve: 50% for a first week, 10 points more for each consecutive week, up to 90%; the rest is paid after review.
    Result
    The person can trace the contribution; the researcher can verify the delivered contents.

  6. Person: stop or withdraw

    Principle
    The person can step back at three levels, and nothing already paid is taken back.
    Apply
    In the participant view, open Stop or withdraw and pick one: skip this round, stop future requests, or withdraw access.
    Watch
    Skipping sends nothing this round. Stopping keeps past data but no new requests. Withdrawing revokes the data licence and returns unreleased escrows; the study's row turns gray.
    Result
    Amounts already received and shares still in review stay with the person.
